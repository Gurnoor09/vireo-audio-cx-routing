Root Cause Mechanism

Keyword-based routing sends delivery and order issues to Billing whenever customer messages contain payment vocabulary such as paid, charged, money, or debited.

26.0% of Billing intake contained explicit delivery/carrier keywords (tracking, courier, not delivered).
16.2% of Billing intake contained return/pickup keywords (reverse pickup, RTO, refund status).
Downstream Operational Friction
632 Recorded inter-team transfers originated from Billing tickets (48.7% of all helpdesk transfers).
20.5% First-response SLA breach rate in Billing due to triage lag while tickets sit in the wrong queue.
